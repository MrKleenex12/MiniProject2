# Reflection: wurmlab_sequenceserver

SequenceServer is a web interface for running BLAST searches, maintained by the Wurm lab. Its timeline is cyclical with 2 gaps of at least three months. Work comes in waves, followed by quieter periods while the maintainers catch up on review.

The longest gap was 4 months, from January to April 2025. The commits before it show Joko Triono actively adding features like "Fold graph by default," so nothing pointed to the project ending. GitHub explains it, though. PR #823 was opened in December 2024 and not merged until June 2025, and issues #824 through #826 got no replies during the gap. This means the gap was a review backlog, not a loss of interest.

The recovery started with a newcomer, Good-soup1, who opened PR #828 to fix issue #826. Then on June 9, 2025, maintainer Tadas Tamosauskas merged the whole backlog in one day. Most of the recovery was the existing team. The project is Active, and its recent themes are security patches and bug fixes, such as "security update to ruby 3.3." Its pace depends on how much time the maintainers have for review.
